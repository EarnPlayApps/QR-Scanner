# QR Scanner Terms of Use

Last updated: 13 October 2026.

Use QR Scanner to scan supported QR codes and barcodes. Verify important results before relying on them. Be cautious when opening links from codes you do not trust. Scan history is stored on your device and can be cleared in Settings. The app may show Google AdMob banner, native, interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads when available. Rewarded ads are optional; a valid completed reward gives one in-app credit to skip one eligible interstitial. Credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred. Ad delivery depends on consent, network connectivity and ad availability. For support, contact earnplayapps@gmail.com.
